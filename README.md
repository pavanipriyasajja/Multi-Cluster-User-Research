# Kubernetes Multi-Cluster UX Research

## What is multi cluster user research?

Deploying, managing and monitoring multiple Kubernetes clusters is <b>challenging</b>. Our user research <b>aims</b> to capture engineers’ experiences and expectations with multi-cluster setups and responsible for multi cluster operations.

Our <b>goal</b> is to make complex multi cluster systems easy to use and turn difficult multi cluster operations into simple human friendly. For more information please read this <a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1uCyHpI5zV1ME9qBtGjLkrIhpl3iXe-zQsn0SSbBiAE4/edit?usp=sharing">proposal doc.</a> 

## Our research will help

<li>Identifies real <b>pain points</b> in multi-cluster operations</li>

<li>Understands engineers’ <b>workflows, needs, and expectations</b></li>

<li>Informs better, <b>user-centered solution</b> design</li>

<li>Reduces complexity and <b>improves usability</b></li>

<li>Makes multi-cluster operations more <b>efficient and easier</b> to manage</li>

## So far what we did: 

We conducted the surveys (<a href="Surveys/Survey-design.md">Design</a>, <a href="Surveys/Analysis-Process-Methods.md">Analysis Methods & Process<a/>) one of the user research method and produced the results in the KubeCon NA 2025 (<a href="https://sched.co/27Nlj">Presentation and video</a> ), Then We transformed the survey findings into a evidence based research to Hybrid Engineer persona and user journey map, highlighting key user needs, challenges, and workflows. We also presented these findings at KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2026.<a href="https://sched.co/2EF5b">Presentation Slides & Video recordings</a> 


## 📌 Status of the Project:

We conducted 1:1 user interviews with engineers to gain deeper insights into their <b>deployment, observability, and maintenance workflows</b> across Kubernetes multi-cluster environments. These interviews helped us uncover detailed operational challenges, pain points, and workflow gaps that were not fully captured in the initial survey. We are presenting these research findings at <b>KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026</b>. <a href="https://tinyurl.com/2ha2549t">Talk information</a> 

## Our next steps:

We are conducting in-depth User interviews to gather more information about multi cluster challenges and expectations from the <b>engineers who deploy applications on top of Kubernetes multi-clusters</b> to understand tools, workflows, and daily practices<a href="https://multicluster.sigs.k8s.io/contributing/user-research/">Learn more from our website</a>
